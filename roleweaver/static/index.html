Your NPCs

Create a profile, then bind a creature in game. NPCs start paused.

Begin by right-clicking an AUTO NPC and choosing Talk To. If direct addressing is enabled, you can also start with its full name, unambiguous first name, or stable ID: Mira: Hello, Mira, can you help?, or Hello, Mira. Each player selects their own NPC; bystanders do not join automatically. See Conversation & Hearing for the current ranges, timeout and line-of-sight rule. Type /rw end to finish. Unaddressed speech outside a conversation gets no AI reply. Paused or DM-controlled NPCs do not answer. Party chat, tells, DM speech and messages starting with // or (( are ignored.

DM possession always stops AI. Unpossessing leaves the NPC paused until you resume it.

Backup and restore

Save profiles, world lore, memories, conversation history and persistent NPC locations. Backups contain player history; keep them private. API keys, passwords and game files are excluded. Restore replaces current data and pauses NPCs.

World lore documents

Public source documents for every NPC. Check Active to include a document in future replies and lore checks. Inactive documents stay saved. Earlier conversations and curated memories may still mention their facts.

Up to 100 documents, 20,000 characters each. Active documents share a 20,000-character prompt budget including headings. New and imported documents start inactive; enable them when ready.

Lore access

Public entries go to every NPC. Faction entries go only to profiles with that faction ID. Private entries go to one NPC. DM-only notes are never sent to the AI. Uncheck Active to exclude an entry from future prompts and lore checks without deleting it. Earlier conversations and memories are retained. A disclosure rule guides what a knowing NPC may say; for absolute secrecy use DM-only.

Spawn at DM

Creates a paused creature at the selected DM using this profile’s saved creature settings and the persistence selected under Manage creature below. Save profile changes before spawning. Release possession to appear in the DM list.

Manage creature

Used by Spawn at DM. For an existing creature, click Apply persistence. Temporary creatures disappear after a module restart; persistent creatures return at their saved location, following the server’s automatic-resume setting.

These actions keep the NPC profile and memories. Move and persistence changes leave AI paused.

Permanent ID: 1–24 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, starting with a letter. Example: captain_beran. Enter the displayed name, such as Captain Beran, in Name.

Controls access to faction lore only; does not change combat allegiance.
