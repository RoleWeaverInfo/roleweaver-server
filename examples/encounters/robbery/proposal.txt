Create a roadside robbery using Rusk (road_robber). He confronts a nearby traveler and demands 30 gold. He is alone, nervous but capable of violence, and may accept an authorized payment down to 10 gold or a credible alternative after an appropriate social check.
Stages: Opening demand; Negotiation after the player answers; Resolution once payment is confirmed, a credible agreement is reached, the player withdraws, or combat resolves. Initiate conversation on trigger entry. Keep dialogue short. Permit persuade, intimidate and bluff, starting DC 15, subject to reviewed permissions. Failed influence attempts must not lead to endless rerolls without a changed approach.
If the player threatens violence or repeatedly refuses while remaining nearby, Rusk warns them and may attack after the authorized grace period and required response. Do not invent endless negotiation prerequisites. Respect withdrawal and pursuit limits. Payment requires exchange confirmation and a native receipt; never invent a transaction or demand a second payment after settlement. No accomplices, guaranteed surrender, forced player actions or invented injuries. The AI DM should oversee the approved scene without an online DM.
