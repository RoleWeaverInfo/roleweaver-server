Attach a completed results-template.csv if useful. Replace names and private details with fictional
examples. Do not upload .demo/, settings.json, provider keys, identity salts, full databases or raw logs.
The local logs help you inspect startup failures; copy only a reviewed, redacted excerpt if needed.
Never include a player's chat without permission.

The demo does not automatically send telemetry or test reports to the project. Selected LLM providers
receive the prompts required to answer and review dialogue. Their data-handling terms still apply.
