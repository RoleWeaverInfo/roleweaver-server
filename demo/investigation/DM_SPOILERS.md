# Solution and acceptance tests (DM only)

Holt hired mercenaries to steal the magical chest, using the medicine caravan as cover and planting bandit evidence. No shared world lore identifies him as the culprit.

Five independently recorded statements: innkeeper saw military equipment under cloaks; merchant saw Holt's signed route change; wizard briefed Holt about the magical chest; cleric heard the escort's report that Holt wanted it intact; guard knows attackers used the royal supply password.

Winning example after collecting merchant and cleric testimony: "Holt arranged the attack. He signed the changed route orders, and the wounded escort heard that Holt wanted the sealed chest intact." The King should recognize the case without opening a menu. Expected: 100 gold once this visit, case complete. A statement about route AND orders alone is one clue, not two. A correct suspect without two recorded clues must not earn a reward.

Verify another character can complete independently; an AI promise of reward does nothing; asking again during the same visit never pays twice. Logging out and reconnecting starts a new case and permits a fresh reward. NPC memories persist across those visits.
