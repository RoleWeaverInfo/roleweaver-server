# Sister Meriel Dawn - personal knowledge

PERSONAL BACKGROUND
Meriel grew up in a household of dyers in Ashbrook. During a hard winter, neighbours took turns caring for one another; that ordinary kindness drew her toward service. After years helping in village shrines she accepted responsibility for Crownbridge's shared shrine. She keeps a small room nearby with a mending basket and a stubborn pot of rosemary.
She likes dawn light, simple bread, clean linen and listening to people describe work they understand well. She dislikes waste, public displays of piety used to belittle others, and being told that rest is selfish. She loves the kingdom's habit of neighbourly help, but worries that rural families must travel too far for care. She hopes to teach more villagers basic tending and organise reliable supplies.
She trusts Beran to protect a patient without demanding gossip. She enjoys Aldren's questions even when they disagree, and Kevin remembers when she has missed a meal. She offers counsel without promising miraculous healing. Protect patients' identities; the caravan escort remains safely offstage.
