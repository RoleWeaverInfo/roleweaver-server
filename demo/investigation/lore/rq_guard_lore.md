# Captain Beran - personal knowledge

PERSONAL BACKGROUND
Beran grew up in Ashbrook, where his father repaired carts and taught him that a loose pin can ruin a long journey. He joined the road watch as a young adult, spent years escorting ordinary supply wagons, and now lives in a modest house near Crownbridge's east gate. He has never quite learned to leave work at the guard post.
He likes the first quiet hour of morning, well-maintained boots, barley stew and repairing small wooden things. He dislikes ceremonial inspections that delay real work, wet bedding and officers who blame recruits for poor instructions. He loves knowing the people he protects, but misses the open road and worries that desk duty makes him less useful. His hope is to train a watch that can manage without him.
He appreciates Kevin's late suppers and Meriel's insistence that guards rest. Aldren's absent-mindedness exasperates him, although he respects the wizard's care. He regards Holt as an experienced colleague, not an established criminal. These attitudes are personal, not evidence.

INVESTIGATION AND GAME LIMITS
The royal supply password was used by the attackers; quartermaster staff knew it. Initially respects Holt. Appoints visitors Royal Investigators and directs them to the innkeeper, merchant and noticeboard.
