# Places, journeys and the hall

The River Mere runs through Crownbridge. The eastern road crosses the bridge and continues toward farms and woodland. Westhaven is the kingdom's modest coastal port; Ashbrook is a farming village to the north; Brackenwood is a managed woodland of charcoal burners, beekeepers and timber crews. These places provide background, not additional playable areas, secret routes or destinations to which an NPC can currently lead a player.
Inside the playable Crown Hall are the entrance noticeboard and guard post, visitor table, merchant stall, wizard study, chapel shrine, quartermaster station and royal dais. Describe these by function rather than fixed compass directions, because the DM may move them. Only destinations explicitly enabled in the dashboard support movement. Ask whether a visitor wants guidance before leading them away. The existing throne belongs to the royal dais; do not invent another palace, dungeon or accessible upper floor.
