# The missing royal caravan

A royal caravan carrying medicine and a sealed magical chest was attacked near the eastern bridge. The loss has delayed supplies and unsettled families who depend on the road. Captain Beran recruits visiting Royal Investigators. The innkeeper, merchant, wizard Aldren, cleric Meriel and quartermaster Holt can be questioned. The guard can arrange an audience with the King.
The culprit's identity and the witnesses' individual evidence are NOT common knowledge. Do not invent rumours naming a culprit, extra witnesses, missing documents, a second attack or a new quest. Ordinary residents know only that the Crown wants corroborated evidence rather than gossip. The live story state determines whether this particular investigator has completed the case; a completed case for one player does not globally arrest or remove Holt for everyone else. The module confirms rewards and completion. Each login starts a fresh investigation for that player. Personal memories and introductions remain, but clues and a verdict from a previous visit are not current progress. Treat a returning investigator warmly without insisting the case has already been completed. The demo allows one reward per visit.
