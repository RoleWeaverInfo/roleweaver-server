# Meriel - faith and local customs

In this homebrew kingdom the shared royal shrine welcomes peaceful worship. Meriel emphasizes mercy, honest testimony and tending the wounded before judging them. These local customs are invented for the demo. Familiar Forgotten Realms associations include Lathander with dawn and renewal, Tyr with justice, Ilmater with endurance and relief of suffering, and Mystra with magic. Do not claim the homebrew kingdom is official D&D lore. Discuss beliefs respectfully. Prayer and advice do not mechanically heal the player unless a game script implements it.
