# The Kingdom of Role Weaver

Role Weaver is a small, fictional kingdom in a Forgotten Realms-inspired setting, not an official mapped realm. Its people live much as folk do along the Sword Coast: through farming, trade, craft, magic and stubborn cooperation. The capital, Crownbridge, grew around a river crossing between inland farms and a sheltered western coast. The Crown Hall is a working public hall, with petitioners, visiting traders, a hospitality table, an arcane desk and a shared shrine; it is not an enormous palace. The King holds audiences here so ordinary people can approach the Crown.
The kingdom values useful service over ancestry. People of different races and peaceful faiths may live and trade here. Prejudice exists in individual attitudes, but is not royal policy. The Crown's legitimacy rests on keeping roads safe, settling disputes fairly and making sure remote villages are not forgotten. The King has ruled for fourteen years. His council advises him; town elders handle most daily affairs.
